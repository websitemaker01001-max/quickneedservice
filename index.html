Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

QuickNeedService collects information needed to process service requests, coordinate with selected providers, handle provider applications, reviews, reports and customer support.

Information may include your name, phone number, service details, address and optional GPS coordinates when you use the GPS feature. Information needed for service coordination may be shared with the selected provider.

Provider applications are reviewed before public listing. QuickNeedService does not sell personal information. Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no internet system can guarantee absolute security.

Call and WhatsApp are third-party services and may have their own privacy policies. You can contact Customer Care to ask questions or request removal of information where applicable.

Policy details may be updated as the service develops.
