QuickNeedService Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we collect. We may collect information you submit when using a service request or provider application, such as name, phone number, service, area, address, timing, landmark, location details and additional job information.

2. How we use information. Information is used to process service requests, help customers and providers coordinate, maintain provider listings, handle reviews/reports and provide customer support.

3. Provider information. Provider applications are reviewed by the administrator. Only relevant approved profile information is made publicly searchable.

4. Location information. GPS is optional in the request form. If you use it, the location information you provide may be stored with the service request so the requested service location can be understood.

5. Sharing. Information may be shared with the selected service provider when it is necessary to coordinate the requested service. We do not sell personal information.

6. Security. Reasonable technical and administrative measures are used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-party services. Call links, WhatsApp and other external services are operated by their respective providers and may have their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data requests. If you have a question about information submitted through QuickNeedService or want to request its removal, contact Customer Care.

9. Policy changes. This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
